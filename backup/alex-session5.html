DR. CLARK What about Akira?
ALEX He was like a brother. I knew him for 4 years — he was a sophomore and used to work as a DJ at the Bronx Palace.
DR. CLARK Did you go to his shows often?
ALEX I didn't like parties that much. They would be very social for me.
DR. CLARK I understand. Why did you go that night then?
ALEX He said it's for all freshmen and I didn't want to feel left out and ..... I had a fight with Dad so I needed a place to go.
DR. CLARK And what happened there?
ALEX It was a dark room. A lot of people. It was my first time there — people drinking, dancing, a lot of people. I was looking for Akira. Then I bumped into Gia — a friend from high school. She started talking about her dropout plan and how she wanted to pursue blogs. I wasn't paying much attention. Then she asked "me."

— the room —

It was blurry. A lot — a lot — of people, all passing by me. I didn't like the touch. Drinks spilling. I couldn't find Akira.
