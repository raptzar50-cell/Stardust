Gia
"what are you doing for the rest of your life?"

the question hit me weird.
like she assumed i had an answer.
like i was supposed to.

i'd been asked about plans before.
maths. university. future.
but "the rest of your life" felt different.
it felt like a lot.

the room was still loud behind her.
i could feel the bass in my chest
but i wasn't listening to the music anymore.

and then i saw her.
across the room.
on the other side of all that noise.
