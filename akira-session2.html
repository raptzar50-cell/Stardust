Pre-Session Note — Dr. Clark

Second session with Akira. He remains resistant but cooperative enough to continue. The goal is to establish trust and begin exploring the events surrounding the incident. He is clearly grieving and angry — both need space.

Extracts — Verbatim

DR. CLARK I hope you understand and cooperate better this session. Our goal is to reach a better place.
AKIRA I said it again and I will say it again — I am not a fan of this. And I'll say it in a polite way: you work for the pigs right now.
DR. CLARK I understand your anger at the police, but do remember this is the route we are in right now.
AKIRA And I do, and I am telling you — whatever you are asking me, like I did yesterday — you yourself said you ain't no therapist, so don't be one. Take these interviews and let me go. I'll tell you whatever you need.
DR. CLARK That's not good for you.
AKIRA What's good for me is my decision to take. I haven't even grieved properly and you guys already want us to comply to you. Just get your info and get moving. Don't try to help me — I have had enough of you guys' help.
DR. CLARK I understand. I would still advise you to lis—

[EXTRACTS ON SOPHIA]

AKIRA I really wanna go home. I don't mind your formal behaviour or care.
DR. CLARK The reason you can talk so openly without any issues is because I am here for your help.
AKIRA Man, we all know the story. Cut the chase.
DR. CLARK No, we can go slow.
AKIRA Ask what you want...
DR. CLARK Tell me about Sophia. How you knew her.
AKIRA How does that help the case? I am not in the mood for small talk.
DR. CLARK I am trying to make details clear.

[Akira and Clark don't speak for a while.]

DR. CLARK Listen — they think this is your route. You saw Diaz. But we can prove it's not. We can show it's how you are pushed, how hard they make your life. We can work towards something.

[There was a silence.]

AKIRA Please, can we move? I don't care as of now.
DR. CLARK So tell me about Sophia and the incident, if you can.
AKIRA What do you wanna know about her?
DR. CLARK You know her background. Anything you wanna tell. How you would describe her.
AKIRA Sophia was for me like a sister.

[He didn't speak for a while after this.]

DR. CLARK Are you OK?
AKIRA Yes... I am.
AKIRA Sophia was my sis. For a long time I was the only person she had. You know she had a rough childhood.
DR. CLARK I understand. Why was it rough?
AKIRA Well, it was.
DR. CLARK If you don't mind, elaborate?
AKIRA Her father was abusive to her mum and her. Her mum eventually ran away from him and came to this city. She tried to raise Sophia all alone.
AKIRA But that's the thing with these steps — they leave us mentally hanged.
AKIRA So her mum wasn't there for her like she needed, but her mum wanted her to be there for her in that sense.
AKIRA And in all this, Sophia had a hard time growing. So that's what was rough — she had no one.
DR. CLARK It was hard on her.
AKIRA So for a long time, she would hang out with me as a safe space and I was their leader — the leader of these outcasts. Like Alex as well.
AKIRA They didn't like Sophia. She was for them a weirdo, because she had a hard time doing things most people couldn't.
DR. CLARK I understand. So for her, you were her brother.

[He started crying as he opened up. I made sure he felt this is a safe space.]

AKIRA But then the incident changed her.
AKIRA It was a lot for a kid.
DR. CLARK I understand, and it must have been very difficult. We don't have to talk about it.
AKIRA I won't. It's with her now.

Post-Session Clinical Note — Dr. Clark

Akira remains hostile towards the process but showed willingness to cooperate when discussing Sophia. His attachment to her is clearly deep — he views himself as her protector and brother figure. He became visibly emotional when discussing her childhood.

He refuses to discuss "the incident" directly. Pushing further at this stage would be counterproductive. The relationship with Sophia is central to understanding his state.
