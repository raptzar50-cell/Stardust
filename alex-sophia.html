Alex Brown — Personal Account

Sophia
in Alex's Eyes

as told in Session 5

They didn't like her.

She was special — different — and they hated that. She would forget her stuff always. Her keys, her books. She couldn't walk well or coordinate herself. During the party she said she forgot to bring the cake for Akira, but unlike others Akira knew her. He was angry the way he usually is, but he was fine with it. Others hated her. Harpy. Limbo. That's what she was to them.

They used to tell stories about her being a slut. She sneaked money out of her mom's wallet to afford meds — she was not on them, she said. She had researched enough and it was okay. It helped her breathe.For me she was special , i thought she was an antisocial extrovert

Her boyfriend was a jerk. I always thought she was with him for the sake of loneliness. I was jealous — but I also felt bad for her. He would always scream at her. She couldn't control her emotions. It was not in her hands. I was also similar — very difficult to work with, I guess.

· · ·

We all were pretending to be someone else. Underneath it was still us — and that's what we hated the most.

We became good friends. Our dorm was the same — she was a floor above. We used to hang out every Saturday. I used to wish I could see her more, but it was fine.

Her boyfriend Sid hated it — that I spent time with her. But I couldn't care. He was a skinhead, not the Nazi kind, just angry. He knew Akira was with me so he didn't bother me.

Deep down I felt she liked me as well. She was with him to not make him feel bad. But that's just me, I guess.

· · ·

One night she was alone. It was a Friday — no one is alone that night. She felt numb. I asked her if she was okay and she said —

"wanna go to the mountain?"

At night? This time? Are you crazy?

Yes, maybe. I don't know.

Not in a million years.

But she said she knew a place. And I thought — maybe she'd fought with Sid. So I said yes.

She took me to the top. There was an observatory.
