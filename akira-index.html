ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

What is E Lit?

E Lit is an interactive story told through the lens of a therapist's clinical archive. Each chapter unlocks a new set of patient files — session transcripts, intake forms, biodata, and notes that slowly reveal something much larger than any single case.

The archive belongs to the Eastbrook Therapeutic Institute. Not everything here is what it seems. Some files have been redacted. Others have gone missing entirely. Read carefully.

Navigate the folders. Read the sessions. Find what they tried to hide.
