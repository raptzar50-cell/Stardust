Pre-Session Note — Dr. Clark

Session focused on Akira's perception of Alex Brown and Sophia. Akira provides vital context regarding Alex's vulnerability, the dynamics within their group, and the relationship between Alex and Sophia leading up to the incident.

[EXTRACTS — DESCRIPTIONS OF ALEX BY AKIRA]

DR. CLARK Alex gifted you that necklace?
AKIRA Yeah, it was his bass pick.
DR. CLARK It's very good.
AKIRA He gave it to me after the party. He met Sophia there.
DR. CLARK The party made him nervous.
AKIRA When wasn't he?
AKIRA He was always too nervous.
DR. CLARK It was nice what you were for him.

[From Alex's recordings — Alex: "He was their leader, you know. The parade head of the misfit toys."]

DR. CLARK He called you their leader.
AKIRA Well, I only wanted things to work.
DR. CLARK What was Alex for you?

[CUTS TO INNER SCENE — ALEX IN AKIRA'S EYES]

He was always a crybaby, ever since his family broke. He was too nervous to work.
I believed he would grow up, grow his wings, man up.
I understand it was rough on him, but he was too pussy.

He would cry like a toddler for small things. They would bully him — place bugs on his shirt.
He would scream, was probably oversensitive. He didn't have to be. I started helping him, protecting him.

Wish he could run better. Used to bring him to the place near the Yankee shop for some hangout with my other friends. We helped him out, I believed.

He was like Sophia in a sense — both mentally just not there, I assume.

That's when he started having words for his issue. She told him about the fog, and he believed he had it.
He would become overwhelmed by thoughts, spiral all day, his hands broken down.
His anxiety needed help.

When they became good friends, I thought the pair was gonna be good. Trauma fixes, I assumed. They were in love. They were in the same boat.

But she didn't want to leave her boyfriend. She was too scared. Sid hated Alex, but couldn't bother cause of me.
When they got closer, I thought they would figure things out.
I never thought things would end this way.

Post-Session Clinical Note — Dr. Clark

Akira's narrative provides crucial perspective on Alex's emotional fragility and how Akira positioned himself as a protector for both Alex and Sophia. His regret over the outcome is palpable.
